Support

How can we help?

We read every message.

Write to us

Email support@orthodoxday.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Support ID shown in the app’s Settings (tap it to copy). For a mistake in a reading, say which day and church you have selected.

Common things

  • Restore a purchase: open the purchase screen and tap Restore Purchase.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Orthodox Day.
  • Request a refund: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Add a widget: touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Customize or the plus/edit control, then choose Orthodox Day.
  • Wrong day’s readings or fast: check the church selected in Settings. Your parish’s calendar is the authority.
